WatchNotes

Privacy policySupport

An AI that watches YouTube with you. WatchNotes opens a side panel next to the video. Google Gemini watches the video itself, frames and audio together, so it can read slides, code and charts on screen. It writes self-contained notes with clickable timestamps, answers your questions as you watch, and saves everything as Markdown, straight into your Obsidian vault if you like.

  • Two modes: watch the video (sees the screen) or read captions (uses less quota).
  • Clickable timestamps jump the player to that second.
  • Notes stay in your browser; optionally written into a folder you pick, or downloaded as .md.
  • Bring your own free Gemini API key. No account, no server, no tracking.
  • Interface in English and Traditional Chinese.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/25qi/watchnotes/issues.
