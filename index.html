An AI that watches YouTube with you, and writes the notes.

Chrome · Edge Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons

Why it is different

Most video summary tools only read the captions, so they are blind to what is on screen. WatchNotes hands the video itself to Google Gemini, frames and audio together, so slides, code, diagrams and charts make it into your notes.

What it does

  • Notes that stand alone: topic sections, concrete steps, tables and takeaways, written in a side panel beside the video.
  • Clickable timestamps: click one and the player jumps to that second.
  • Ask while you watch: mention a time such as 07:20 and the answer is grounded in that moment.
  • Your own thoughts: "My note" saves your text verbatim, never sent to the AI.
  • Two modes: watch the video (sees the screen), or read captions (uses much less quota).
  • One library: every video's notes stay in your browser. Reopen, keep asking, or download as Markdown.
  • Obsidian friendly: pick a folder, such as your vault, and notes are written there in real time.
  • Languages: interface in English and Traditional Chinese; notes in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese or Japanese.

Getting started

  1. Install WatchNotes from the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons (links will appear here once published).
  2. Get a free Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it into the extension's settings. The settings page has a step-by-step guide.
  3. Open any YouTube video, click the WatchNotes icon, and press Start by watching.

Privacy in one paragraph

No account, no developer server, no analytics, no tracking. Your key, settings and notes stay in your browser. Requests go straight from your browser to Google's Gemini API with your own key; on Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Read the full privacy policy.

哪裡不一樣

多數影片摘要工具只讀字幕，看不到畫面上的東西。WatchNotes 把影片本身交給 Google Gemini，畫面和聲音一起看，投影片、程式碼、圖表的內容都會寫進你的筆記。

功能

  • 自足的知識筆記：主題段落、具體步驟、表格與行動建議，寫在影片旁的側邊面板。
  • 可點的時間戳：點一下，播放器就跳到那一秒。
  • 邊看邊問：問題裡帶上 07:20 這種時間，AI 就針對那一段回答。
  • 記下自己的想法：「記想法」原封不動存進筆記，不會送給 AI。
  • 兩種模式：看影片（讀得到畫面），或讀字幕（省很多額度）。
  • 全部筆記一個地方：每部影片的筆記都存在瀏覽器，隨時打開、繼續問，或下載成 Markdown。
  • 支援 Obsidian：選一個資料夾（例如你的 vault），筆記會即時寫進去。
  • 多語言：介面支援英文與繁體中文；筆記可用英文、繁體中文、簡體中文或日文撰寫。

開始使用

  1. 從 Chrome 線上應用程式商店或 Edge 附加元件安裝 WatchNotes（上架後連結會放在這裡）。
  2. 到 aistudio.google.com/apikey 免費申請 Gemini API key，貼到擴充套件的設定頁；設定頁有逐步教學。
  3. 打開任何一部 YouTube 影片，點工具列的 WatchNotes 圖示，按 看影片開始。

隱私，一段話說完

不用帳號、沒有開發者伺服器、沒有分析與追蹤。你的 key、設定和筆記都只存在你的瀏覽器。請求從你的瀏覽器用你自己的 key 直接送到 Google Gemini API；使用免費層時，Google 可能用送出的內容改進產品。完整內容請看隱私權政策。
